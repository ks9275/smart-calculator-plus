Privacy Policy

Smart Calculator Plus  ·  Last Updated: May 29, 2026

1. Overview

Smart Calculator Plus ("the App") is a free utility app that provides a standard calculator, scientific calculator, unit converter, percentage/discount calculator, and date calculator.

The developer is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains clearly how the App handles information.

2. Information the App Collects Directly

The App does not collect any personal information directly.

Specifically, the App does NOT:

  • Require login or account registration
  • Collect personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Transmit any values or expressions you enter to external servers
  • Access your location
  • Request sensitive device permissions (camera, microphone, contacts, etc.)
  • Operate any servers or store your data remotely

All calculations are performed locally on your device and are never transmitted externally.
