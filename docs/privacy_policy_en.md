# Privacy Policy

**App Name:** Smart Calculator Plus  
**Last Updated:** May 28, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Smart Calculator Plus ("the App") is a free utility app that provides a standard calculator, scientific calculator, unit converter, percentage/discount calculator, and date calculator.

The developer is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains clearly how the App handles information.

---

## 2. Information the App Collects Directly

**The App does not collect any personal information directly.**

Specifically, the App does NOT:

- Require login or account registration
- Collect personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Transmit any values or expressions you enter to external servers
- Access your location
- Request sensitive device permissions (camera, microphone, contacts, etc.)
- Operate any servers or store your data remotely

All calculations are performed locally on your device and are never transmitted externally.

---

## 3. Third-Party Service — Google AdMob

To keep the App free, it displays banner advertisements through **Google AdMob**, an advertising platform operated by Google LLC.

For the purpose of delivering and measuring ads, AdMob may process the following types of information:

| Processor | Information That May Be Processed |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|
| Google AdMob | Advertising ID |
| Google AdMob | Device information (model, OS version, etc.) |
| Google AdMob | IP address |
| Google AdMob | App usage data (ad impressions, clicks, etc.) |
| Google AdMob | Approximate location (derived from IP) |

This information is processed directly by **Google**, not by the App developer.  
You can review Google's data practices here:

- Google Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- AdMob Partner Policies: [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

### Ad Personalization

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings.  
Path: **Settings → Google → Ads**
